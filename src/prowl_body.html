        Why can't I find vintage on Google?

        Because most of it isn't there. Goodwill's auction site and Facebook Marketplace sit behind logins and scripts that Google doesn't index. Search "Roseville jardiniere" and you'll get eBay, Etsy, and a wall of Pinterest — you won't get the one sitting at $18 on shopgoodwill right now.

        Prowl runs your search across 24 vintage marketplaces at once — including the ones Google skips. Free, no account, built by a collector who got tired of typing the same search into eight sites every morning.

        How collectors use it

        • Your grail list — save the pieces you've hunted for years. Prowl remembers them and keeps them one tap away.
        • Search All — one term, every marketplace, including the ones Google can't reach.
        • Price Check — one tap, one tab: what pieces actually sold for on eBay in the last 90 days. Real prices, not asking prices. For rare pieces that trade twice a year, one more tap opens three years of deep comps.

        Is Prowl free?

        Yes. No account. Your search list stays on your device.

        Does it work on my phone?

        Yes. Built mobile-first. Add to home screen. Allow pop-ups.

        How does Price Check work?

        One tap opens eBay's sold listings for your term — actual sale prices from the last 90 days, the most accurate read on what a piece is worth right now. Asking prices lie; sold prices don't.

        What if nothing sold in 90 days?

        That happens with genuinely rare pieces. Price Check has a second button for three years of deep comps via eBay's Terapeak research — free with any eBay account. Open it in your browser rather than the eBay app, or the date range gets dropped.

        Welcome to Prowl

        Prowl builds one search for 24 vintage marketplaces and puts them all on one screen. Four things to know:

        1

        One hunt, every marketplace

        Type a hunt and hit Hunt. Every marketplace lands as a row on one screen. Tap a row to open that one, or Open all to launch the lot.

        2

        Open all needs pop-ups allowed

        24 tabs at once looks like a pop-up storm to a browser. Allow pop-ups for this site if you use Open all — tapping rows one at a time always works.

        3

        Lock for DAILY

        Lock a hunt in Den and it joins your daily prowl. DAILY in the footer runs everything you've locked.

        4

        Three themes

        Tap the icon top-right to cycle light, dark, and uranium — a retro blacklight mode, where the mushroom finally glows.
