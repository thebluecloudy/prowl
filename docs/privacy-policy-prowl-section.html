Prowl, Prowl Alerts and Field Guides

Prowl is our free search tool for vintage collectors, at owlandtwigvintage.com/pages/prowl, and the Prowl app you can add to your home screen. Part of it runs on Shopify and part on our own service, hosted by Cloudflare. This section explains what Prowl collects, where it goes and how long we keep it. It applies together with the rest of this Privacy Policy. Questions or requests: hello@owlandtwigvintage.com.

What stays on your device

  • Your Prowl data. Your hunts (saved searches), saved and liked finds, finds you watch for a price drop, saved research, Field Guides, your last five photo checks (with a small copy of the first photo), the place you set for Near me, the group trip you are on, and your display and email settings are kept in your own browser. We do not receive them unless you use a feature below that sends them. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • Sign-in. If you sign in, your browser keeps your Prowl Alerts email address and a sign-in key, and a store sign-in key that lasts 30 days.
  • Cookies on iPhone and iPad. When you open the Prowl app page in Safari on an iPhone or iPad, Prowl sets up to two cookies, only for that page: prowl_c2 (your hunts and Prowl settings, such as your chosen marketplaces and the city you set, if they fit) and, when you are signed in, prowl_c3, a one-time code that is not your email address or your sign-in itself. They carry your settings and your sign-in into the app when you add it to your home screen. The app copies them once and deletes its own copy, then trades the code with our service for your sign-in; the code works once, runs out after 15 minutes and stops working if you sign out every other device. Safari writes prowl_c2 again each time you visit or leave the page, and it expires 7 days after your last visit. Older versions of Prowl kept your sign-in itself in a cookie named prowl_c1; Prowl now deletes it the next time you open the page. Apart from that code, they never leave our shop's own website.
  • Page visits. The Prowl page uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits, page speed and which website sent you. It sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device and does not identify you.
  • The Chrome extension. If you install the Prowl extension, it keeps your Prowl Alerts email and sign-in key in the extension, copied from the Prowl page so you do not sign in twice. It sends your searches to our Prowl service, or opens marketplaces in new tabs.

Searching

  • Search words. When you search or open a hunt, the words you type are sent to our service, which looks them up for you on marketplaces such as ShopGoodwill, The Salvation Army, eBay, Etsy, Chairish, Poshmark, Vinted, Grailed, HiBid and Craigslist and returns the listings. Those lookups are made from our servers, not from your device. eBay and Etsy are asked through their official services. Results are kept for a short time in a cache by search words, not tied to you.
  • Links that open a marketplace. Buttons that open a marketplace in a new tab (such as Search All) send your search words to that site directly from your browser, under that site's own policy.
  • Affiliate links. Some marketplace links carry our affiliate code, and we may earn a commission if you buy. The note at the bottom of the Prowl page names the marketplaces where that is the case.
  • Anonymous counts. To learn what collectors look for, we keep daily counts of search words, Field Guide subjects and road trip destinations: how many different visitors used each and how many times. To count a visitor once, we use a one-way code made from your IP address and the date. It changes every day and is deleted within three days. The counts are kept for up to 60 days. They are never linked to your name, email address or account. We skip words that look like an email address, a web link or a long number, and destinations that start with a house number.
  • Usage limits. To keep Prowl free and stop abuse, we limit how many searches, photo checks, Fair price checks, research notes, Field Guides, place lookups and shared emails each visitor and each network can make in a day. These limits are daily counters that hold your IP address and the network it belongs to, and are deleted within about two days. Limits that are about an email address (signing in, syncing your likes and saved finds, Prowl Pro checks and limits, and how many shared emails one address can receive) are counted against a one-way code of the address instead, never the address itself, on the same schedule.
  • Error records. When a search fails, our service records what failed and the search words, with no address or account, for up to 30 days, so we can fix it.

Signing in and Prowl Pro

  • Signing in with your Owl & Twig account. Prowl uses Shopify's own sign-in through our Shopify app. Shopify tells Prowl who you are: your customer number and email address go to our service. Your first name is shown in the greeting on your screen, and goes with any find you post to a group trip, where the trip's members see it. We may link your customer number to your Prowl Alerts record. Log out from the Prowl header, or Den, then Settings, then Account.
  • Signing in by email. You can also sign in with a 6-digit code or a link we email you. The code lasts 10 minutes and is stored only as a one-way code. A sign-in link lasts a day. The "Open Prowl" links in our emails carry a sign-in pass that works once and lasts 3 days, never your sign-in key; on a device that is not signed in, Prowl asks before it signs in. Once a pass is used we keep a note of it until it runs out, so it cannot be used again.
  • Signing out everywhere. Den, then Settings, then "Sign out every other device" signs you out of Prowl on every other phone and computer, ends the store sign-ins Prowl kept, and makes the links in emails you already have stop signing anyone in. The device you use stays signed in. Prowl cannot log you out of your Owl & Twig store account: a phone or computer that is still logged in to the store can sign back in through it, so log out of the store there, or change your store password, to stop that.
  • Prowl Pro. Prowl Pro is bought through our Shopify checkout, so Shopify handles your payment and we never see your card number. To unlock Pro we look up your Prowl Pro orders in Shopify by your email address, the Prowl email you typed on the order, or your store customer number. We keep a short note of the answer (whether you have Pro, and your last order's number, date, amount and receipt link) for up to 7 days. If we give you Pro as a gift, we keep a one-way code of your email address, or we add your address to a private list of free Pro members in our service's settings. We may email you about a gift, and during a free month we send a welcome and a reminder before it renews, through Resend. If you tap "Stop gift emails", we keep a one-way code of your address so we send no more.

Prowl Alerts, the weekly letter and phone alerts

  • Your Prowl Alerts record. If you sign up, we store your email address, your hunts, your email settings (how often, what time, your time zone, which marketplaces, muted hunts, words to skip, a price ceiling, a pause date, and, if you use local pickup, your ZIP code and distance), your sign-in keys, whether you have Prowl Pro, and your store customer number if you signed in through the store. If you use local pickup, your ZIP code is sent to eBay with your hunts so it can show pieces you can pick up.
  • Alert emails. We search the marketplaces for your hunts on a schedule and email you new finds through our email provider, Resend. We keep the listings waiting for your next email and a list of listings already sent so we do not repeat them. Every email has an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing stops the emails and clears the waiting and already-sent lists, but keeps your hunts so you can turn emails back on.
  • The weekly letter. On Sundays we email Prowl Alerts members a short letter about their week. To write it we keep a running tally of the week's new finds and the best one, reset each week. When the letter is written it also reads your likes, and for a hunt that found nothing it may ask eBay once whether a shorter search finds something. We keep a copy of the letter for the app for 21 days. You can turn the letter off on its own from the link in it or in your settings.
  • Product updates. Now and then (a few times a year at most) we email Prowl Alerts members a short note about what is new in Prowl, through Resend. Members with emails off, or who have not saved a hunt, do not get it. Each one has our postal address, a "Stop product updates" link (also the one-click unsubscribe in your mail app) and a link to stop all Prowl emails; there are no tracking pixels. To send each person one copy, we mark your record with which update you were sent.
  • Likes and ignored words. If you are signed in, the finds you like (the heart) and the words you ask Prowl to ignore are kept with your record so they follow you to your other devices.
  • Saved finds (Prowl Pro). Pro members' saved finds follow the account. Each one is kept for a year from the day you saved it, with a copy of the listing's photo in our storage, because marketplaces take their photos down once a piece sells. For finds on eBay, Etsy, ShopGoodwill and The Salvation Army, we look at the listing up to about once a day to mark it sold or ended. Photos are shown only through private links that expire.
  • Facebook Marketplace finds (Prowl Pro). If you have Prowl Pro and have set a local-pickup ZIP code, each day our service sends the words of some of your hunts (up to 10 searches a day, taken in turn), an area point and a search distance to Apify, which searches Facebook Marketplace for them from its own servers, logged out: it never uses your Facebook account or anyone else's, and no cookies are sent. The area point is the middle of a square of about 10 km around your ZIP code, shared with everyone nearby; your ZIP code, address and email address are not sent to Apify. To find that point, your ZIP code is sent once to Google's Geocoding service, and the point is kept for 90 days. For a hunt you set to search the whole US, the search covers the widest area Facebook allows around that point, and we also search, by hunt words alone, for listings that ship. Of each listing we keep only its title, price, photo, town, its distance in miles from the area point and link, for about a week (8 days), kept by hunt words and area rather than by person (with each search's counts: how many listings Facebook returned and how many were set aside as too far or missing a hunt word), and we show them in Prowl and in your alert emails. The words you type in the Store's Facebook search box go to Apify the same way. We count each member's Facebook searches for the day and remember when each of your hunts was last searched (so each gets its turn), under a one-way code rather than your address (that record is removed when you delete your account, or after 60 days unused), and if you choose to search a hunt across the whole US we keep that choice with your Prowl Alerts settings. We never keep seller details. Apify itself holds each search's raw results, which can include a seller's public name, in our Apify account for its short storage period (about a week) before deleting them; we do not read or copy those fields. When you open a listing, your browser goes to Facebook under Facebook's own policy.
  • Estate sales this week (Prowl Pro). Under NEAR ME, then Estate sales, Prowl lists the estate sales open in the next 7 days near the town you type or where your device is. The town is looked up with Google as in Near me; your position is rounded to about 1 km, and your hunts and time zone go to our service with it. Our service asks EstateSales.net for the sales near that rounded point and reads each sale's own page there, so EstateSales.net receives only the point and the distance (or the town, when its list does not answer), never your hunts, name or email address; your hunts are matched against the sales on our side. The lists are kept for a few hours by place and each sale's page for a day, not tied to you. If you have Prowl Pro and a local-pickup ZIP code, your daily Prowl email can carry an "Estate sales near you" section: new sales open in the next 7 days near the centre of your ZIP code (found once with Google's Geocoding service and kept by ZIP for 90 days) that mention one of your alert hunts. The day's sales near a ZIP are kept for about a day and a half by place, shared by the members there; we keep the numbers of the sales already sent to you (for up to 30 days) so the same sale is not sent twice. You can turn it off in Den, then Settings, or with the "Stop estate sales in these emails" link in the email. Its "Plan my week" link carries the sales' numbers and your ZIP code, never your email address or sign-in, and opens the plan for anyone it is forwarded to until those 7 days end. Plan my week sends the start and the sales' locations to the public OSRM route service to order the stops, as Road trip does; a planned day can be opened in Road trip, which works on your device the same way. Estate sales on the way: when a Prowl Pro member plans a Road trip, the route (points rounded to about 1 km, starting a mile from where you set off, or for a loop the start and its stops), the trip's day, your time zone and your hunts go to our service, which asks EstateSales.net for the sales around a few points along the route; EstateSales.net receives only those points, rounded to about 7 miles, and a distance, never your hunts or who you are. Nothing about the trip is kept; the sales are kept by place as above. An estate sale you add to a trip stays on your device.
  • Pounce. If you set up a Pounce quarry, we keep its description, skip words, size and price range, the photo link you chose, whether you want "maybe" matches, and up to 8 listings you told it were not it. Our service checks your hunt's new listings against it with Meta's Muse AI service, sending each listing's title, price and photo and your reference photo. It remembers which listings it has already checked, keeps listings it has not reached yet for the rest of the day, and keeps up to 20 recent matches for you.
  • Phone alerts. If you turn on "Alert this phone", we keep your phone's notification address and keys (up to 5 phones), with a label for the kind of device (such as iPhone or Android), under a one-way code of your email address. Notifications are sent through your browser maker's push service (for example Google or Apple), encrypted so that service cannot read them. They name the piece, its price and marketplace, your hunt, why it matched, and the listing's photo. Your name and email address are never readable in them.
  • Changing your email address. We keep up to three earlier addresses with your record, and a forwarding note for each for 90 days, so links in older emails still work. We send one notice to the old address.
  • Email this. If you email a find to someone, the address you type, the note and the find's photo card go through our service to Resend, which sends that one email under its own policy. We do not keep the address; only a daily counter under a one-way code of it, so one address cannot be sent too many. Every such email carries our postal address and a link that stops Prowl emailing that address again; if it is used, we keep only a keyed one-way code of the address, so we can refuse later sends.

Feedback

  • Send feedback. If you send feedback (Den, then Settings, then Send feedback, or the link at the foot of the Prowl page), we keep what you wrote, your rating, the date, the Prowl version and whether you were on Free, Prowl Pro or not signed in, for 180 days. We keep your email address with it only if you are signed in and tick "OK to email me back", so we can reply; otherwise there is no address and no IP address with it. If you type an email address or a phone number into the boxes, we take it out before the note is kept or sent to us, so use the reply box to hear back. A copy is emailed to us at Owl & Twig through Resend, with your address as the reply address only when you asked for a reply. Deleting your Prowl Alerts account removes your address from any feedback you sent, including an address you used before you changed it. To have a note removed sooner, email us.

Photo checks, Fair price, Research and Field Guides

  • Check a photo. The photos you choose (up to 3) and any note you type are sent through our service to Meta's Muse AI service, to identify and price the piece, and the first photo to eBay, to find similar listings. When switched on, the first photo also goes to Google Cloud Vision, to find the same picture on the web, and to Google Lens through SerpApi: for that, the photo is placed in our storage behind a private link that works for 2 minutes and is deleted as soon as SerpApi answers. If you scan a whole shelf, the photo goes only to Muse, to read what is on the shelf. We do not keep your photos. For 30 minutes we keep a one-way code made from your IP address and the photo, so checking the same photo again is free.
  • Check with Google Lens. If you tap this, your photo is placed in our storage behind a private link that works for 15 minutes, and a new tab opens Google Lens with that link, so Google fetches the photo under its own policy. We delete the photo within two hours.
  • Fair price, Research and Field Guides. The listing details, item name or subject you type are sent to Meta's Muse AI service, which may search the web. Answers are kept in a cache by item or subject, not tied to you, for up to 30 days. Field Guides are AI-written research and may contain mistakes, so check the sources before you buy.

Near me, road trips and group trips

  • Your location. Near me and Road trip use the location your device gives (only if you allow it) or a town you type. That point, and any destination or stops you type, are sent from our service to Google Maps Platform (Places, Routes and Geocoding) to find shops and plan the drive. Your starting point (to about 100 m) and the stops may go to the public OSRM route service to work out the order. When you only ask which town you are in, Prowl rounds your position to about 1 km. Near me and Road trip send Google your position to within about 10 metres. If you tap Open in Google Maps or Apple Maps, your browser sends the start, stops and destination to that service. Answers are cached by place, not by person: up to a day for shops, routes and place suggestions, 30 days for a town. The map pictures come from OpenStreetMap, which your browser asks for the area shown, and the map code loads from Cloudflare's cdnjs.
  • Group trips (Prowl Pro). A saved trip keeps its organizer and members (as store customer numbers, or a one-way code of a Prowl Alerts email address), the starting town (never a street), the destination as Google names it, the shops on the route, any stops you typed (as you typed them, which could be an address), and which stops are marked as visited. The organizer can share an invite link: anyone signed in to Prowl who has it can see the trip and join it until the trip ends. If the organizer adds someone by email, we look the address up by a one-way code and never keep the address. Nobody is put on a trip that way: the invitation waits for 30 days under that code, and for someone who uses Prowl under NEAR ME, then Trips, where they can join it or decline it. The organizer is not told whether the address has a Prowl account. To make that work, each Prowl sign-in (through the store or by email), and each use of group trips while signed in, keeps a one-way code of your email address linked to your member id. We also keep a list of the trips you are on. If you change your Prowl Alerts email address, your trips, invitations and this link move with you.
  • Trip finds. A photo you post to a trip is shown to the trip's members, with your first name from your Owl & Twig account, the item, its price, the photo check's verdict and fair price, and the stop and town. Photos are stored privately and shown only through links that expire after an hour. The trip, its finds and their photos are deleted 7 days after the trip closes, or at once when the organizer deletes it. If you leave a trip, the finds you posted stay with it until then. If you delete your Prowl Alerts account, the finds you posted and their photos are deleted at once.

Who else is involved

  • Shopify runs our shop, the store sign-in and Prowl Pro checkout and orders.
  • Cloudflare hosts our Prowl service, its storage and caches, processes requests (including IP addresses), and provides the page's visit counts.
  • Resend sends our emails.
  • Meta provides the Muse AI service, and TypeSafe provides the background helpers (it receives hunt words and listing details).
  • Apify searches Facebook Marketplace for Prowl Pro members' hunts. It receives the hunt words and an area point of about 10 km, never a name, an email address or a Facebook account.
  • Google provides Maps Platform for Near me and road trips, and, when switched on, Cloud Vision for photo checks. SerpApi fetches Google Lens matches when that is switched on.
  • EstateSales.net lists the estate sales for Prowl Pro members' searches and the estate sales section of their daily emails. It receives a point rounded to about 1 km, or the centre of a ZIP code, or for estate sales on a Road trip a few points along the route rounded to about 7 miles, and a distance (or, when that list does not answer, the town and state), from our servers.
  • OSRM, a public route service, receives route points to order the stops. OpenStreetMap provides the map pictures.
  • Your browser also loads the page's fonts from Google Fonts and the map code from cdnjs (Cloudflare), and listing photos straight from each marketplace, so those services see your IP address.
  • eBay receives search words, the photo you check and, for local pickup, your ZIP code. Etsy and the other marketplaces receive search words or listing links, from our servers.
  • We do not sell your search words, hunts, location or photos.

Deleting your data

  • Delete my Prowl Alerts account. In Den, then Settings, "Delete my Prowl Alerts account" removes your Prowl Alerts record and everything kept with it: your email address and hunts, settings and ZIP code, sign-in keys, waiting and already-sent listings, likes and ignored words, saved finds and their photos (under your current and any earlier address), Pounce quarries and matches, the weekly tally and letter, phone alerts, the Prowl Pro note, earlier addresses and their forwarding notes, the link to your store customer number, and your group trips: you are taken off every trip you are on, with the finds and photos you posted; a trip you organized passes to the next member, or is deleted if nobody else is on it; your list of trips, waiting trip invitations (including ones you have not answered) and the one-way code that links your address to your member id for trip invitations are deleted too. Your device is signed out of Prowl Alerts; a store sign-in on it stays until you log out. It does not cancel Prowl Pro billing: cancel that in your Owl & Twig account first.
  • What it does not remove. A trip you passed on stays with its other members, as do their finds. A gift record or a request to stop gift or shared emails (a do-not-email list), the one-way code of a store sign-in address that differs from your Prowl Alerts address (it points only at your customer number), and the anonymous counts, which hold nothing that identifies you, also stay. Facebook Marketplace finds, estate sale lists and ZIP code area points are kept by hunt words and place, not by person, and run out on their own (8 days, a day, and 90 days). To have any of these removed, email us.
  • On your device. Clearing your browser data, or removing the app or extension, removes what is kept there.
  • Requests. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it removed or corrected, email hello@owlandtwigvintage.com from the address you use with Prowl.

How long Prowl keeps things

  • Until you delete it: your Prowl Alerts record, the list of listings already sent, likes and ignored words, Pounce quarries, the listings Pounce has checked and its recent matches, phone alerts.
  • Until you delete your Prowl Alerts account: the trip-invitation link to your member id and the list of your trips.
  • Until you ask us to remove it: gift records and requests to stop gift emails.
  • A year from saving: Pro saved finds and their photos.
  • 180 days: feedback you send (and your address with it, only if you asked for a reply).
  • 90 days: forwarding notes for an earlier email address; the area point for a ZIP code (Facebook finds and estate sales).
  • 60 days: anonymous daily counts.
  • 30 days or less: cached AI answers (Fair price, photo prices, Research, Field Guides) and town lookups, kept by item, subject or place; a waiting trip invitation; error records; the numbers of the estate sales already sent to you.
  • 21 days: the weekly letter's copy for the app.
  • 8 days: Facebook Marketplace finds for a hunt and area.
  • 7 days: the Prowl Pro note; a group trip, its finds and photos after the trip closes.
  • Two to three days: the estate sales near a ZIP code gathered for that day's emails (about a day and a half, by place); daily usage counters, the daily visitor code, the confirm link for a new sign-up, the note that an email's sign-in pass was used, and a copy of your last alert email's listings (so it can be sent again).
  • A day or less: sign-in links and codes, a pending email-change link, Pounce listings waiting to be checked, cached listing details, search results, shops and routes, estate sale lists (a few hours) and each sale's page.
  • Two hours or less: a photo sent to Google Lens. Photos for photo checks are not kept.

How Prowl uses AI

We know many collectors, resellers and shoppers are wary of AI, so here is exactly what it does in Prowl, what it does not do, and how to turn it off.

  • Only when you tap. These features use AI, and each runs only when you press its button. Fair price reads a listing and nearby listings and suggests whether it looks like a deal, fair or high. Check a photo suggests what a piece may be and what it may be worth. Research this piece looks up a piece's maker, marks, lookalikes and sold prices on the web. Field Guides research a subject you type and write a short guide with sources.
  • Pounce, if you set it up. Pounce checks new listings for your hunt against the piece you described and alerts you to likely matches.
  • In the background. A few small helpers run by themselves: they match pieces from our shop to your hunts, suggest which marketplaces suit a hunt, check whether a new hunt looks like a collectible name, and, where we have it switched on, filter out search results and emailed finds that do not match your search or look like reproductions.
  • What AI does not do. It does not make decisions about you, build a profile of you, set the prices of our shop, or buy, sell or message anything for you. It never sees your name, email address or payment details. We send it only what a feature needs: the words you searched, listing details and photos, your description, or the photo you chose.
  • It can be wrong. AI answers are labeled as AI, show their sources where there are any, and are a starting point for your own checking, not an appraisal or a guarantee.
  • Your choice. Prowl Pro members can turn AI off with one switch: Den, then Settings, then AI helpers, then Turn off. The switch is kept on the device you use, so turn it off on each device. On that device the Fair price, photo, Research and Field Guide features are then hidden and the background helpers stop, and if you are signed in, your emailed finds are no longer sorted by AI, your alert emails no longer show shop pieces matched by AI, and Pounce stops checking listings (and cannot be set up) until you turn AI back on. Searching, your hunts, your Den and Prowl Alerts keep working. Everyone, free or Pro, controls the tap-only features by not tapping them.
  • Who provides the AI. Meta's Muse service powers Fair price, Check a photo, Research, Field Guides and Pounce. TypeSafe powers the background helpers (shop matches, where to look, hunt-name checks and result filtering). They handle what we send under their own policies.
