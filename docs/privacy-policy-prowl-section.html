Prowl, Prowl Alerts and Field Guides

Prowl is our free search tool for vintage collectors, found at owlandtwigvintage.com/pages/prowl. Part of it runs on our own service, hosted by Cloudflare, in addition to Shopify. This section explains what Prowl collects and where it goes. It applies together with the rest of this Privacy Policy.

  • Kept on your device. The searches you save (your hunts), saved finds, saved Field Guides, and your display and email settings are stored in your own browser. We do not receive them unless you sign up for Prowl Alerts. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • Searching. When you search or add a hunt, the words you type are sent to our service, which looks them up for you on marketplaces such as ShopGoodwill, eBay, Etsy, Chairish, Poshmark, Depop, Vinted and Grailed and returns the listings. Those lookups are made from our servers, not from your device. We also use automated services, including TypeSafe, to check that listings match what you searched for. Results are temporarily kept in a cache by search words, not tied to you. Buttons that open a marketplace in a new tab (such as Search All and Facebook) send your search words to that site directly from your browser. Some eBay links carry our affiliate code, and we may earn a commission if you buy.
  • Anonymous search counts. To learn what collectors are looking for, we keep daily counts of search words and Field Guide subjects: how many different visitors used each and how many requests were made. To avoid counting the same visitor twice we use a one-way code made from your IP address and the date. The code changes every day, cannot be turned back into your address, and is deleted after two days. Counts are kept for up to 60 days. We do not link these counts to your name, email address or account, and we skip words that look like an email address, a web link or a long number.
  • Usage limits. To keep Prowl free and prevent abuse, we limit how many lookups, photo checks and Field Guides each visitor can make in a day. These limits use your IP address with a daily counter that is deleted within about two days.
  • Photo checks, Fair price checks and Research. If you use Check a photo, the photo you choose and any note you type are sent through our service to eBay (to find similar listings) and to Meta's Muse AI service (to identify and price the piece). We do not store your photo. If you use Fair price or Research, the listing details or item name are sent to Meta's Muse AI service, and the answer may be kept in a cache by item for up to 30 days, not tied to you. These services handle the information under their own policies.
  • Field Guides. When you make a Field Guide, the subject you type is sent to Meta's Muse AI service, which researches the web to write it. Guides are kept in a cache for 30 days by subject, so the same subject returns the same guide, and are not linked to you. Field Guides are AI-generated research and may contain mistakes, so check the sources before you buy.
  • Prowl Alerts. If you sign up, we store your email address, the hunts you choose, your preferences (how often, which marketplaces, muted hunts, price ceiling and, if you use local pickup, your ZIP code and distance), a sign-in code that keeps your devices signed in, whether you have Prowl Pro, and a list of listing links we have already emailed you so we do not repeat them. We use your hunts to search the marketplaces on a schedule and email you new finds, through our email provider, Resend. Every email has an unsubscribe link; unsubscribing stops the emails but keeps your hunts so you can turn them back on. You can delete your Prowl Alerts account and everything stored for it at any time with "Delete my Prowl Alerts account" in the Den settings, or by contacting us.
  • Prowl Pro. Prowl Pro is bought through our Shopify checkout, so Shopify handles your payment details and we never see or store your card number. To unlock Pro features we match your email address to your Prowl Pro order in Shopify.
  • Who else is involved. Cloudflare hosts our Prowl service and processes requests, including IP addresses. Resend sends our emails. Meta provides the Muse AI service. TypeSafe provides automated listing checks. eBay and Etsy provide listings through their official services. We do not sell your Prowl search words, hunts or photos.

How Prowl uses AI

We know many collectors, resellers and shoppers are wary of AI, so here is exactly what it does in Prowl, what it does not do, and how to turn it off.

  • Only when you tap. Four features use AI, and each runs only when you press its button. Fair price reads a listing and nearby listings and suggests whether it looks like a deal, fair or high, with a price range. Check a photo suggests what a piece may be and what it may be worth. Research this piece looks up a piece's maker, marks, lookalikes and sold prices on the web. Field Guides research a subject you type on the web and write a short guide with sources.
  • In the background. A few small helpers run by themselves: they match pieces from our shop to your hunts, suggest which marketplaces suit a hunt, check whether a new hunt looks like a collectible name, and, where we have it switched on, filter out search results and emailed finds that do not match your search or look like reproductions.
  • What AI does not do. It does not make decisions about you, build a profile of you, set the prices of our shop, or buy, sell or message anything for you. It never sees your name, email address or payment details. We send it only what a feature needs: the words you searched, the listing details, or the photo you chose.
  • It can be wrong. AI answers are labeled as AI, show their sources where there are any, and are a starting point for your own checking, not an appraisal or a guarantee. Check the sources before you buy.
  • Your choice. Prowl Pro members can turn all AI off with one switch: Den, then Settings, then AI helpers, then Turn off. With AI off, Prowl sends nothing to an AI service. The Fair price, photo, Research and Field Guide features are hidden, the background helpers stop, and your emailed finds are no longer filtered by AI. Searching, your hunts, your Den, Daily and Prowl Alerts keep working. Everyone, free or Pro, controls the tap-only features by simply not tapping them.
  • Who provides the AI. Meta's Muse service powers Fair price, Check a photo, Research and Field Guides, and TypeSafe powers the background helpers. They handle what we send under their own policies; see the section above on who else is involved.
