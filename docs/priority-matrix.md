# Prowl priority matrix (PIE)

*Made 2026-10-12, after the competitor teardowns (Cat On Chair, Gemli, ThriftAI, VendorsMap), the trademark search and
Matt's own testing (the Couroc tray, Novi estate sales, the Craigslist 404). Update the Status column as things ship.*

Each idea is scored 1 to 10 on **Potential** (how much it could move Prowl), **Importance** (how much it matters to
collectors and to getting on the App Store) and **Ease** (how quickly it can be built). **PIE** is their average.
**Impact** = (Potential + Importance) ÷ 2. The quadrant lines sit at 6.5 on both axes:

- **Quick win:** high impact, easy. **Big bet:** high impact, hard. **Fill-in:** easy, smaller payoff. **Not now:** hard
  and smaller payoff.
- **Part of #14** marks App Store requirements: they score low alone, but the App Store app can't ship without them.

The scores are Claude's judgement, for discussion: change any you disagree with.
