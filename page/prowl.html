      How Local works
      1. Set your city first. Type your city, like Novi, or a Craigslist region code like detroit or sfbay, and tap Save. It stays on this phone and feeds Craigslist, Facebook and the store finder.
      2. Find stores near you. Tap a type, like antique stores, estate sales or flea markets, and a map search opens around you. Pick Google Maps or Apple Maps once and Prowl remembers. Garage sales opens this week's garage and moving sales on Craigslist for your city.

      4. Search just one local site. Type a hunt up top or tap one of yours, then come back here and tap Craigslist or Facebook Marketplace at the bottom to search only there.

        What is Prowl?

        Prowl by Owl & Twig is a free search tool for vintage collectors. Type a piece once and it builds that search for 29 marketplaces on one screen, including ShopGoodwill, Facebook Marketplace and Craigslist. It is built by Matt at Owl & Twig Vintage, a family vintage shop in Michigan.

        Which marketplaces does Prowl search?

        eBay, Etsy, Mercari, Mercari Japan, Poshmark, Depop, Grailed, Ruby Lane, Chairish, 1stDibs, Reverb, Whatnot, Vinted, Bonanza, eCrater, AbeBooks, AptDeco, OfferUp, Facebook Marketplace, ShopGoodwill, Salvation Army, EstateSales.net, HiBid, LiveAuctioneers, CTBids, Heritage Auctions, Invaluable, Craigslist and eBay Sold, for what pieces actually sold for: 29 in all. ShopGoodwill, eBay, Etsy, Chairish, Poshmark, Vinted, Grailed and Salvation Army results show right on the page; the rest open in their own tab. Instagram sits beside them as a hashtag search.

        How do I find antique shops and estate sales near me?

        Open the Den and tap Local. Tap a store type, like antique stores, thrift stores, estate sales or flea markets, and a map search opens around you in Google Maps or Apple Maps. Save your city there too, and Craigslist and Facebook search that area.

        Is Prowl free?

        Yes. Searching is free and needs no account, and your search list stays on your device. Your Den keeps 5 hunts of your own for free, or up to 100 with Prowl Pro. Email alerts are optional: free for 1 hunt every Monday, or Prowl Pro for daily alerts.

        Does it work on my phone?

        Yes. Built mobile-first. Add it to your home screen, and allow pop-ups if you use Open all. On a phone, Facebook opens in your browser so the search sticks, so log in to Facebook there once.

        How does Price Check work?

        One tap opens eBay's sold listings for your term — actual sale prices from the last 90 days, the most accurate read on what a piece is worth right now. Asking prices lie; sold prices don't.

        What if nothing sold in 90 days?

        That happens with genuinely rare pieces. Price Check has a second button for three years of deep comps via eBay's Terapeak research — free with any eBay account. Open it in your browser rather than the eBay app, or the date range gets dropped.

        How Prowl works

        Prowl builds one search for 29 vintage marketplaces and puts them all on one screen. The basics:

        1

        Search everywhere at once

        Type what you are hunting and tap Hunt. Every marketplace lands as a row; tap one to open it, or Open all (allow pop-ups for this site). Near me opens a map of antique, thrift and vintage shops around you.

        2

        Your favorite hunts

        Type into + add a hunt on the home screen. Prowl checks ShopGoodwill, eBay, Etsy, Chairish, Poshmark, Vinted, Grailed and Salvation Army right away, with photos, and marks what is new since you last looked. The Den holds every hunt you have saved.

        3

        DAILY

        DAILY in the footer runs all your locked hunts in one go: what is new since yesterday first, then live listings for each.

        4

        Prowl Alerts by email

        One email with new finds for your hunts, as often as you pick. Free for 1 hunt, every Monday. Prowl Pro is free for the first month, then $7 a month for up to 25 hunts every day and your Den. Cancel any time. Set it up, see your plan and receipts, or turn emails off in Den › Settings. Tap Sign in or sign up to use your Owl & Twig account: we email you a 6-digit code, no password, and the same sign-in works on your phone and computer.

        5

        Check a photo

        At a sale, tap Photo in the bar at the bottom, then Take a photo. One free check a day, 25 with Prowl Pro. Prowl names the piece, notes visible flaws and gives a fair price range, with eBay look-alikes.

        6

        Make it yours

        Aa makes the text bigger. The icon top-right cycles Light, Blue hour, Midnight and Uranium, a retro blacklight mode. Some links to eBay earn Owl & Twig a small commission, at no cost to you.
