        What is Prowl?

        Prowl by Owl & Twig is a free search tool for vintage collectors. Type a piece once and it builds that search for 25 marketplaces on one screen, including ShopGoodwill, Facebook Marketplace and Craigslist, which a regular Google search rarely reaches. It is built by Matt at Owl & Twig Vintage, a family vintage shop in Michigan.

        Which marketplaces does Prowl search?

        eBay, Etsy, Mercari, Poshmark, Depop, Grailed, Ruby Lane, Chairish, 1stDibs, Reverb, Whatnot, Vinted, Bonanza, eCrater, AbeBooks, AptDeco, OfferUp, Facebook Marketplace, ShopGoodwill, EstateSales.net, HiBid, LiveAuctioneers, Invaluable and Craigslist, plus eBay sold listings for pricing. ShopGoodwill, eBay, Etsy and Chairish results show right on the page; the rest open in their own tab. Instagram sits beside them as a hashtag search.

        How do I find antique shops and estate sales near me?

        Open the Den and tap Local. Tap a store type, like antique stores, thrift stores, estate sales or flea markets, and a map search opens around you in Google Maps or Apple Maps. Save your city there too, and Craigslist and Facebook search that area.

        Is Prowl free?

        Yes. No account. Your search list stays on your device.

        Does it work on my phone?

        Yes. Built mobile-first. Add it to your home screen, and allow pop-ups if you use Open all. On a phone, Facebook opens in your browser so the search sticks, so log in to Facebook there once.

        How does Price Check work?

        One tap opens eBay's sold listings for your term — actual sale prices from the last 90 days, the most accurate read on what a piece is worth right now. Asking prices lie; sold prices don't.

        What if nothing sold in 90 days?

        That happens with genuinely rare pieces. Price Check has a second button for three years of deep comps via eBay's Terapeak research — free with any eBay account. Open it in your browser rather than the eBay app, or the date range gets dropped.

        How Prowl works

        Prowl builds one search for 25 vintage marketplaces and puts them all on one screen. The basics:

        1

        Search everywhere at once

        Type what you are hunting and tap Hunt. Every marketplace lands as a row; tap one to open it, or Open all (allow pop-ups for this site). Facebook USA runs the term in every big metro.

        2

        Your favorite hunts

        Type into + add a hunt on the home screen. Prowl checks ShopGoodwill, eBay, Etsy and Chairish right away, with photos, and marks what is new since you last looked. The Den holds every hunt you have saved.

        3

        DAILY

        DAILY in the footer runs all your locked hunts in one go, with live listings for each.

        4

        Prowl Alerts by email

        One email each morning with new finds for your hunts. Free for 1 hunt, every Monday. Prowl Pro is free for the first month, then $7 a month for up to 25 hunts every day and your Den. Cancel any time. Set it up, see your plan and receipts, or turn emails off in Den › Settings. Tap Sign in or sign up to use your Owl & Twig account: we email you a 6-digit code, no password, and the same sign-in works on your phone and computer.

        5

        Get the app

        Prowl works best installed. Tap Get the app, then on iPhone use Share › Add to Home Screen; on Android or a computer tap Install. It is also in Den › Settings.

        6

        Check a photo

        At a sale, tap Photo in the bar at the bottom, then Take a photo. Two free checks a day, 15 with Prowl Pro. Prowl names the piece, notes visible flaws and gives a fair price range, with eBay look-alikes.

        7

        Make it yours

        Aa makes the text bigger. The icon top-right cycles light, dark and uranium, a retro blacklight mode. Some eBay links earn Owl & Twig a small commission, at no cost to you.
