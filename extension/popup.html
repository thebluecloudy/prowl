Add live listings with Prowl Pro

Prowl Pro ($7 a month, first month free) adds live listings in this window, Field Guides, your Daily Prowl and photo checks.

On Prowl, open the Den, then Settings, and sign in to Prowl Alerts with your Pro email. This extension picks that up the next time you visit Prowl in this browser.

Tip: highlight text on any page and right-click it, or type prowl and a space in the address bar. Right-click a photo to identify it with Google Lens.
