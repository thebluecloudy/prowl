A bonus for Prowl Pro members

The Prowl toolbar search, live listings and right-click search are included with Prowl Pro ($7 a month, first month free).

On Prowl, open the Den, then Settings, and sign in to Prowl Alerts with your Pro email. This extension picks that up by itself the next time you visit Prowl in this browser.

Tip: highlight text on any page and right-click it, or type prowl and a space in the address bar. Right-click a photo to identify it with Google Lens.
